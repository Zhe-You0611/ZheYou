01About

I am a master’s student in the School of Mathematics at East China University of Science and Technology.

My research explores spectral and geometric questions on graphs, including eigenvalue problems and discrete curvature. I also work on dispersive estimates for wave equations on lattices.

02Research Interests

Spectral graph theory

Dirichlet and Steklov eigenvalues; Faber–Krahn-type inequalities on trees.

Discrete curvature

Lin–Lu–Yau and Bakry–Émery curvature; structural and extremal questions on graphs.

Analysis on lattices

Discrete wave equations and sharp dispersive estimates.
